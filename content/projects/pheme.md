name: Pheme
slug: pheme
one_liner: Writes meeting notes for Vietnamese teams without joining the call.
status: Building
tier: side
visibility: private
started: 2026-04
order: 5
featured: false
problem: >
  Meeting notetakers send a bot into your call. It shows up in the participant
  list, it needs permission, and in a bilingual meeting it produces a transcript
  that is wrong in both languages. Vietnamese teams end up taking notes by hand
  in a meeting that was supposed to be recorded.
approach: >
  Record from the device instead of the call. Two audio streams, your microphone
  and the system output, captured locally and sent up in chunks for transcription
  and summary. No bot, no calendar permission, nothing for the other side to
  notice. The trade is that it only works on the machine you are sitting at.
stage: >
  Private beta as of September 2026, with the first outside testers using it on real
  meetings.
vision: >
  A meeting in two languages should produce one set of notes that both sides
  trust. Get that right in Vietnamese and the same approach travels to every
  market that codeswitches, which is most of them.
evidence:
  - claim: One beta user sent 1,437 transcription requests in a single day, 2026-09-09
