problem: >
  Vietnamese builders can follow AI tutorials for months and still ship nothing anyone pays for.
  Courses teach tools in isolation, so the gap between learning and a live product stays.
  Most of them work alone, with no deadline and nobody checking whether the thing works.
approach: >
  Runs one cohort on a fixed four week clock, ending in a public Demo Day.
  Every session ends with a brief, and every brief is graded against a working product.
  The trade-off is scale: a full cohort rules out one-to-one review for every builder.
stage: >
  As of 2026-09-12 cohort 01 is in week four, with session seven on 2026-09-13.
  The Campus platform, the transcript AI chat and the assignment flow all run in production.
  The Replay product and its affiliate program are specified and priced but not yet built.
vision: >
  In three years the default way to learn building is a paid cohort with deadlines.
  Graduates become the distribution channel, earning commission on the programs and tools they helped build.
  If that holds, teaching pays for itself and the alumni network outgrows the original course.
evidence:
  - claim: 140 enrolled, 120 signed into Campus, 61 submitted at least one assignment as of 2026-09-12
  - claim: Nine sessions from 2026-08-23 to Demo Day on 2026-09-20
