name: Echoly
slug: echoly
one_liner: Translates YouTube audio into your language while the video plays.
status: Paused
tier: side
started: 2026-05
order: 6
featured: false
problem: >
  Subtitles make you read instead of watch, and auto-translated ones are often
  worse than nothing. A talk in a language you do not speak stays closed to you
  even when the whole internet is one click away.
approach: >
  A Chrome extension that taps the page audio, translates it, and speaks it back
  in near real time. Two tiers: a realtime one measured in hundreds of
  milliseconds, and a cheaper standard one for anything not live. You bring your
  own API key, so the cost is yours and the margin is not hidden.
stage: >
  The extension works and the source is open. Development has been paused since June 2026.
vision: >
  Language stops deciding which talks you get to watch. The idea still holds;
  the timing did not.
evidence:

    source: https://github.com/sonpiaz/echoly
  - claim: 13 languages supported at the last release, 2026-06-18
