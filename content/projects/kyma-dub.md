problem: >
  A creator with a good video in one language reaches a fraction of the audience it could. A studio
  dub is expensive, and subtitles lose the viewers who will not read them.
approach: >
  One command chains the steps a studio does by hand: extract the audio, transcribe it, translate
  the text, synthesise a new voice, and align it to the timing of the original so the speech lands
  where the speaker's did.
stage: >
  Public and working today as a command line tool and an MCP server.
vision: >
  Language stops being the reason a video only reaches one country.
