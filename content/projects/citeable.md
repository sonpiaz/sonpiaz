name: Citeable
slug: citeable
one_liner: Shows a brand how AI answer engines describe it, then drafts the fix.
status: Paused
tier: side
visibility: private
started: 2026-06

featured: false
problem: >
  More buying decisions now start with a question typed into an AI assistant, and most brands have
  no idea what those assistants say about them or who they recommend instead. Traditional search
  tools measure rankings on a results page that fewer people see.
approach: >
  Ask a fixed set of real buyer questions across several AI answer engines, record every answer,
  and have a judge score whether the brand is mentioned and where. Then turn the gap into a draft:
  content written to answer the exact question the brand is missing from.
stage: >
  Private. It worked end to end and was verified on a demo brand in June 2026, then paused so the
  work could go into the products that already had users. Not launched.
vision: >
  Being cited by an AI assistant becomes a channel a team manages on purpose, measured every month
  the way search rankings are today.
evidence:
  - claim: 216 sampled AI answers in the first study; the demo brand appeared in 0 percent of them while its category leader appeared in 92 percent
  - claim: n/a, not launched
