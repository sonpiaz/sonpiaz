problem: >
  SaaS founders who want an affiliate program choose between enterprise platforms and building
  the whole thing themselves. The incumbent platforms price for companies that already
  earn real revenue through partners. A founder with no program yet gets quoted five figures a
  year before anything is proven.
approach: >
  A brand adds one tracking script. A link becomes a click, a click becomes a lead, a lead becomes
  a sale, and each step is recorded so a commission can be held, checked and paid. The brand never
  builds the tracking, and partners see the same record the brand sees.
stage: >
  Production runs at api.affitor.com and serves the brands already on it. Fixes ship when a live
  customer hits something, most recently a Stripe Connect callback bug on 2026-09-12. Affitor LLC is the parent company for later projects, including Kyma API.
vision: >
  Attribution should be a signed record both sides can audit, not a cookie owned by one party.
  If that holds, partner payouts stop depending on trusting whoever controls the tracking script.
  The verifiable attribution ledger built here is the piece most likely to outlive everything else.
evidence:

  - claim: "Six systems in production: tracking API, dashboard, CLI, skills library, public directory, docs"
