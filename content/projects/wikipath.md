problem: >
  Vietnamese history is full of figures connected by marriage and lineage, and there is no tool that
  lets someone follow those connections with a source behind each step. The information exists,
  scattered across biographies and encyclopedias.
approach: >
  Search a name, see a visual family tree, and ask for the path between two people. Every fact links
  back to where it came from, and the data is open so anyone can correct it.
stage: >
  Live at wikipath.app as an early version with a seed dataset, open for corrections.
vision: >
  Genealogy of public figures becomes open, sourced data rather than something repeated without a
  citation.
evidence:
  - claim: Seed dataset of about 6,000 people, 250 biographies and 1,500 portraits
