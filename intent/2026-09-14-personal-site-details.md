# Personal site details

**Problem:** The site presents the main products in inconsistent orders, repeats
product explanations across pages, and does not yet show the tools, portrait,
older writing, or contribution activity that complete the public profile.

**Outcome:** The site has one generated Stack page, a consistent product order
and first-party product marks, a self-hosted portrait, two cleaned canonical
articles, and a self-hosted contribution graph refreshed by daily builds. Every
HTML and machine-readable surface stays synchronized and verified.

**Constraints:** Keep Astro static, preserve existing URLs, use one global
stylesheet, avoid runtime third-party requests for images or data, validate all
build-time network input, keep public content free of private data, and require
review before merge.

**Out of scope:** Changing or removing the Substack originals, adding a runtime
service, changing repository protection, manually deploying, or merging this
work.

**Open questions:** None.
